## 📣 Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it privately using **one** of the following methods:

- 📧 Email: [security@veradeveloper.com](mailto:security@veradeveloper.com)
- 📂 GitHub: [Open a security issue](https://github.com/veradeveloper/offensive_security_tool/issues) (label it with `Security`)

---

## 📋 What to Include in Your Report

To help us triage and respond effectively, please include:

- ✅ A clear and concise description of the vulnerability.
- ✅ Steps to reproduce the issue (PoC if applicable).
- ✅ Impact assessment (e.g., local/remote exploit, privilege escalation, DoS).
- ✅ Any logs, screenshots, or payloads relevant to the report.

---

## 🔄 Disclosure Timeline

- ⏱️ **Acknowledgement** within *48 hours*.
- 🔧 **Fix progress updates** every *7 business days*.
- ✅ Notification upon *patch release or resolution*.
- ❌ If the report is invalid or out of scope, we will explain why.

---

## 🔏 Confidentiality

All reports are kept **strictly confidential** until a fix or mitigation is in place. Public disclosure before coordinated resolution is discouraged and may pose risks to users.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This tool is intended **solely for ethical and authorized security testing**. Unauthorized use, including scanning systems without permission, is illegal and unethical.

Please refer to the [Security Policy](LICENSE.md) and [Readme](README.md) for responsible use policies.

Thank you for contributing to the security and integrity of this project.
